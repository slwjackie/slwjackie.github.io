01 ELECTRODE GEOMETRY · SCHEMATIC
PROPULSION / MULTIPHYSICSOngoing

Electrically controlled
solid propellants

How does electrode geometry shape the response before ignition?

I study electrode design for electrically controlled solid propellants (ECSP). The computational framework couples electrochemistry, heat transfer, and condensed-phase decomposition to study the preflame response.

  • Preflame modeling
  • Electrode design
  • Multiphysics
Research brief +

Approach. Geometry screening and controlled straight-electrode studies examine spacing, width, and anode-to-cathode area ratio within a shared preflame solver.

Current focus. Connect computational trends with experimental calibration. Model parameters remain literature-based and have not yet been calibrated to my propellant samples.

Research direction. Understand the relationship between electrode geometry, current distribution, and thermal response as a basis for lower-voltage ignition studies.

02 MULTILEVEL GRIDS · SCHEMATIC
NUMERICAL METHODS / SCIENTIFIC MLOngoing

Adaptive neural multigrid

When should a classical solver use a learned component?

I investigate how learned components can be integrated into classical multigrid. My current work studies neural smoothing and a solver world model for deciding when to reuse, rebuild, or fall back to classical components.

  • Elliptic PDEs
  • Neural smoothing
  • Adaptive solvers
Research brief +

Approach. Study solver decisions on synthetic elliptic problems with heterogeneous coefficients, including anisotropic and structured cases.

Evaluation. Compare against a tuned classical baseline while accounting for learning, setup, and inference costs. Practical speedup is a research objective, not an established result.

Research direction. Learn when adaptation is useful while preserving a dependable classical fallback. This project is a numerical-methods testbed, not a reacting-flow CFD solver.

View repository ↗adaptive-multigrid
03 PREDICTION + VERIFICATION · SCHEMATIC
RELIABLE ML / CONSERVATION LAWSOngoing

Certified advice for
learning-augmented solvers

Can a solver check learned advice before trusting it?

I study when a numerical solver can accept a learned prediction and when it should fall back to a classical method. The project pairs a Burgers conservation-law testbed with a separate study of zero-dimensional hydrogen autoignition.

  • Error certificates
  • Burgers equation
  • H₂ kinetics
Research brief +

Stage A · Conservation law. Deterministic error accounting for a one-dimensional inviscid Burgers PDE, relative to a same-grid Godunov method under the documented arithmetic assumptions.

Stage B · Chemical kinetics. A separate constant-volume H₂/O₂/N₂ kinetics ODE testbed uses Cantera as a reference and explores restricted interval-based verification.

Scope. Full ignition-trajectory certification and practical acceleration remain research goals. Stage B does not simulate propagating shocks or full detonation dynamics.

  • Turbine rim seals

TURBOMACHINERY / SEALINGProposed

Can wheel-space swirl help predict the sealing performance of rotor-side swirlers?

Reading published work on turbine rim seals led me to formulate a question: how should the Ingress Wave Model (IWM) fluctuation-amplitude parameter ΦA be adjusted when a swirler changes the wheel-space flow?

I would like to investigate whether measured wheel-space swirl can complement annulus-swirl-based scaling to improve predictions of sealing effectiveness and required sealing flow.

Proposed study +

Analysis plan. If suitable baseline and rotor-side swirler datasets become available, quantify the relationship between measured wheel-space swirl and ΦA. Compare multiple flow coefficients where the data permit.

Validation plan. Evaluate predictions at operating conditions excluded from calibration, accounting for measurement uncertainty. The question is whether adding wheel-space swirl improves prediction beyond the existing annulus-swirl-based scaling.

Possible experimental work. If access and facilities permit, participate in measurements of sealing effectiveness and radial wheel-space swirl. The scope would be refined with the supervising professor around available data, equipment, and ongoing lab research.

Context and status. This proposal was formulated independently from published literature. My interaction with Prof. Sungjin Song was a meeting; I have not held an internship or research appointment in his lab. No data analysis, experimental measurements, or validation results have been completed for this proposal.
